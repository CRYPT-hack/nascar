# Original Stock Car Asset Pack

Ten original, low-poly oval-track stock-car models in Wavefront OBJ format. They are inspired by the *category* and proportions in the supplied references, but do not recreate any real driver, team, sponsor, manufacturer body, logo, or livery.

## Contents

- `stock_car_01.obj` through `stock_car_10.obj` — individual game assets
- matching `.mtl` files — paint, glass, wheel and trim materials

Each model uses metres (approximately 5.1 m long × 2.05 m wide) and faces forward on +Z. Import each OBJ with its matching MTL in the same directory. The meshes are clean, separate assets, with baked-in low-poly visual detail; add colliders, LODs, physics, and UV texture atlases in your engine or DCC tool as appropriate.
